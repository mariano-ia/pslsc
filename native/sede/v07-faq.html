FAQ

Before you board.

Why aren’t we playing in our own stadium?
Our stadium at Walton & One is under construction in Port St. Lucie. Until it opens, we play our home matches at Treasure Coast Field.
How long will we play here?
For the 2027 season. Our stadium is expected to open at the beginning of 2028.
Does my Ticket Deposit work here?
Yes. Ticket Deposits secure priority access to 2027 season tickets, and the 2027 season is played here. Your $35 is applied to your balance at seat selection.
How do I get there from Port St. Lucie?
About 25 minutes by car via US-1 or I-95 (exit 129). On matchdays, a free shuttle leaves the Port St. Lucie Civic Center 90 minutes before kickoff.
Where do I buy tickets?
Online, on the club’s ticketing site. Every ticket link on this page takes you straight there.
