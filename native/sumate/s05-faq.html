Before you join

The honest questions.

Why $35 for the deposit?
The deposit is intended to secure your priority place in line for 2027 season tickets. The $35 deposit will be applied to your overall ticket balance at the time of seat selection.
Can I still become a Founding Member?
No. Port St. Lucie SC has reached its 1,000 Founding Member milestone, and the Founding Member group is now complete. Ticket Deposits are still available and will give deposit holders priority access when season tickets go on sale.
What does a Ticket Deposit provide?
A Ticket Deposit secures your priority place in line to purchase 2027 season tickets and will be applied to your overall ticket balance at the time of seat selection.
Why should I place a deposit?
2027 season tickets will be made available based on a priority list. Placing a deposit gives you priority access before tickets become available to the general public.
Do I need to place a deposit for each seat I intend to purchase?
No. Only one deposit is needed per account. Each account can purchase up to five season tickets online. If you are interested in more than five season tickets, please contact us for assistance.
Does a Ticket Deposit guarantee season tickets?
No. A Ticket Deposit secures your place in the priority line but does not guarantee season tickets. Availability will depend on inventory when seat selection begins.
When will season tickets be available?
Season ticket sales will launch once the club finalizes the details for the 2027 season. Deposit holders will receive information about their priority purchase window before tickets become available to the general public.
How will I choose my seats?
Deposit holders will be contacted with a specific timeframe and instructions for seat selection.
What seating options will be available?
Deposit holders will have the opportunity to select from the seating categories available when seat selection begins.
How much are season tickets?
Season ticket pricing starts as low as $140.
Where will Port St. Lucie SC play in 2027?
Port St. Lucie SC will begin its inaugural professional season at an interim home venue. Additional venue details will be shared once finalized. The club’s new stadium at Walton & One is expected to open at the beginning of 2028.
