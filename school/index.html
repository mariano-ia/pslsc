Your questions answered

What ages is the Soccer School designed for?

The Soccer School welcomes children ages 5–13.

Does my child need previous soccer experience?

Children with or without previous soccer experience are welcome.

What will my child learn?

Children will learn soccer fundamentals through play while developing confidence, important values, and social skills.

Who will be coaching the children?

Experienced coaches working with a shared methodology.

Does pre-registering commit me to anything?

No. Pre-registering is free and doesn’t commit you to anything. You’ll be the first to hear about location, schedule and pricing, and you’ll get to register before registration opens to everyone.
